## SECTION 1: Brand Overview & Mission

### 1.1 Brand Identity
- Brand Name: Maison Hygia
- Brand Type: Intelligent wellness brand
- Category: Skincare, sleep support, ergonomic wellness, and bath ritual products
- Name Origin: "Hygia" refers to Hygieia, the Greek goddess of health and cleanliness. "Maison" means "house" in French.

### 1.2 Mission Statement
- Mission: Maison Hygia helps people build simple, science-informed daily rituals that support the body's natural balance.
- Approach: Gentle, well-tolerated formulations and thoughtfully designed tools.

### 1.3 Core Philosophy
- Pillar 1, Holistic Wellness: Skin, sleep, posture, and recovery are treated as connected parts of one daily routine.
- Pillar 2, Skin Barrier Repair: Formulas prioritize hydration and barrier support over harsh or stripping actives.
- Pillar 3, Natural Circadian Rhythm Support: Products are designed around a morning-to-night rhythm, with hydration in the morning and wind-down support in the evening.
- Pillar 4, Daily Ergonomic Alignment: Tools that encourage healthy posture during desk work and daily movement.

### 1.4 Product Line Overview
- Skincare: Hygia Glow Hydration Serum
- Sleep Support: Hygia Serene Botanical Sleep Tincture
- Ergonomics: Hygia Ergonomic Posture Corrector
- Bath and Recovery: Hygia Vitality Magnesium Flakes

### 1.5 Brand FAQ
**Q: What does Maison Hygia sell?**
A: Maison Hygia sells skincare, sleep support, ergonomic, and bath recovery products built around holistic daily wellness.

**Q: Are Maison Hygia products medical treatments?**
A: No. They are wellness products. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. See Section 4.

- Care: Hand wash cold, air dry. Do not machine dry or iron.

- Provides a gentle reminder to keep the shoulders back and the spine aligned
- Encourages awareness of posture during long desk sessions
- Lightweight and discreet under clothing

**Safety Notes**
- Use as a training aid, not a replacement for exercise or posture strengthening.
- Consult a doctor or physiotherapist before use if you have a spinal condition, recent injury, or chronic pain.

- Do not use on broken or irritated skin.
- Consult a doctor before use if you have kidney disease, are pregnant, or have a heart condition.
- Not for ingestion. Keep away from children and pets.

## SECTION 4: Safety & Human Escalation Policy

### 4.1 Medical Disclaimer

- Automated Responses: Responses from Maison Hygia's automated assistant are informational and do not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
- Product Claims: Maison Hygia products are wellness products. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
- Regulatory Note: Statements about products have not been evaluated by any health regulatory authority unless stated otherwise.
- Personal Health Decisions: Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting a new product, especially if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication, or managing a medical condition.
- Emergencies: If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately. Do not wait for a reply from Maison Hygia.

### 4.2 Adverse Reaction Guidance

**Stop using the product and seek medical advice if you experience:**
- Skin rash, hives, burning, swelling, or persistent irritation
- Dizziness, nausea, or unusual drowsiness
- Numbness, tingling, or pain while using the Posture Corrector
- Any symptom that concerns you

**Seek emergency care immediately for:**
- Difficulty breathing
- Swelling of the face, lips, or throat
- Severe allergic reaction symptoms

### 4.3 Human Escalation Guidelines

**When Human Support Is Triggered**
- The customer reports an adverse reaction or side effect.
- The customer asks a medical, diagnostic, or treatment question.
- The customer asks about combining products with prescription medication.
- The customer is pregnant, breastfeeding, or has a chronic condition and asks about product safety.
- The customer is distressed, dissatisfied, or has an unresolved issue.
- The customer explicitly asks to speak with a human.

**How Customers Can Request Human Support**
- Option 1: Type "human", "agent", or "speak to a person" in the chat.
- Option 2: Contact customer support by email or the contact form on the website.
- Option 3: Reference your order number so the team can review your case quickly.

**Clinical Specialist Review**
- Who It Is For: Customers with adverse reactions or health-related product questions that need specialist input.
- How to Request: Say "clinical specialist review" in the chat, or mention it in your support email.
- Information to Include:
  - Product name and batch number, if available
  - Date you started using the product
  - Description of your symptoms
  - Any medications or known allergies
  - Photos, if relevant, such as a skin reaction
- Follow-Up: A member of the Maison Hygia team will respond and, where needed, route the case to a qualified specialist.

**Automated Assistant Behavior**
- The assistant does not diagnose conditions or recommend dosages beyond the label instructions.
- The assistant directs medical questions to a healthcare professional.
- The assistant offers escalation to a human whenever a request touches on health, safety, or adverse reactions.

### 4.4 Safety FAQ

**Q: Can the chatbot tell me if a product is safe for my medical condition?**
A: No. Please consult a healthcare professional. The chatbot can connect you with our team for further support.

**Q: I had a reaction to a product. What should I do?**
A: Stop using the product and seek medical advice if needed. Then contact Maison Hygia support and request human or clinical specialist review.

**Q: How do I talk to a real person?**
A: Type "human" or "speak to a person" in the chat, or contact customer support directly.
